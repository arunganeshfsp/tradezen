Strategy Lab

A workbench for intraday strategy experiments on paper. The first study — Day-Range Breakout — scans at 10:15, marks bullish setups (buyer dominance > 55%) against the day's high and bearish setups (seller dominance > 55%) against the day's low, and activates a setup only when price crosses that reference level. Descriptive study only — no orders are placed.
