How to read the matrix
Delta — buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated volume in the candle. Shade strength follows size relative to the other columns on screen.
Max Δ / Min Δ (Candle) — the highest and lowest the running delta reached while the candle formed. A candle that closes near its Min Δ after a high Max Δ shows buying that faded.
Cum Delta — running total from the first session loaded; coloured by that candle's delta. Read its direction rather than its level.
Delta/Vol % — how one-sided the candle was: |delta| ÷ volume. 50%+ 30–50%
Session Δ — running delta from the 09:15 open, reset each day. positive negative
Volume — at least 1.5× the average of the previous 20 columns.
How the split is estimated — the broker feed has no trade-side flag, so each 1-minute bar's volume is assigned from its own direction (up/down method) or from where it closed within its range. Treat values as an estimate of pressure, not exact order flow.
NIFTY FUT uses the near-month contract (the index itself has no volume). Just after expiry, earlier sessions of the new contract show lighter volume.
